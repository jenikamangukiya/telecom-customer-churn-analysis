# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges in the telecom industry. This project analyzes customer behavior using Python to identify the major factors influencing customer churn and provides actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retaining existing customers is significantly cheaper than acquiring new ones. This project aims to identify why customers leave the telecom company and determine the factors that contribute most to churn.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains information for **7,043 telecom customers**, including:

- Customer Demographics
- Contract Type
- Internet Service
- Monthly Charges
- Payment Method
- Customer Status
- Churn Category
- Churn Reason

---

# 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project explores the following business questions:

- Customer Status Distribution
- Contract Type vs Customer Churn
- Internet Type vs Customer Churn
- Monthly Charges vs Customer Status
- Customer Tenure vs Customer Status
- Churn Categories
- Top Churn Reasons
- Payment Method vs Customer Churn

---

# 💡 Key Findings

- Approximately **27%** of customers churned.

- Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts** had the highest churn rate (**45.8%**).

- Customers with **higher monthly charges** were more likely to churn.

- Customers who churned had a **median tenure of only 10 months**, while retained customers had a median tenure of **42 months**.

- **Competitor offers and service dissatisfaction** were the leading churn drivers.

- Certain **payment methods** showed higher churn percentages than others.

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts.
- Improve customer onboarding during the first year.
- Review pricing for premium plans.
- Improve Fiber Optic service quality.
- Analyze competitor offerings and improve customer retention strategies.
